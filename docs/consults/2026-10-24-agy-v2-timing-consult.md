# Antigravity consult on ARM9 v2's timing shape (2026-10-24, plan mode, gemini-4-argon-high)

The brief: docs/consults/2026-10-24-v2-timing-consult-brief.md. The answer follows verbatim (its file links point at the V2 worktree and the scratch clone of the Pocket core).

### Executive Summary

At 104 MHz (`9.615 ns` period) on `5CSEBA6U23I7`, cross-quadrant paths between `zap_dcache` and `arm9v2_core`/`arm9v2_execute` pay **0.55–0.68 ns** in global-clock skew + $t_{su}$ ([`s9-final-paths.json`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/docs/consults/arm9v2/s9-final-paths.json#L8-L35), [`detail_Slow_1100mV_100C_Model.txt`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/docs/consults/arm9v2/s8-final-seed2/detail_Slow_1100mV_100C_Model.txt#L76-L93)). That leaves an **effective data-delay budget of ~8.93 ns for 0 ns slack (or ~9.93 ns to hit `-1.0 ns` slack)**. Today the top five path families all sit in a tight **11.56–11.80 ns** data-delay wall (`-2.56` to `-2.87 ns` slack).

### 1. Why the Early/Late Split + Miss Policy Cost 1.6 ns vs. S6, and Why Pocket Closes at 88.7 MHz

#### A. Schedule Equivalence first: ARM9v2's 2-clock late pickup is *already* the Pocket schedule
A common misconception is that Pocket's 1-stall unshifted load-use (`afwd`) has an extra pipeline stage relative to its cache compared to ARM9v2. **It does not.** The two pipelines are stage-for-stage identical on a D-cache hit:
* **Analogue Pocket** ([`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L1045-L1050), [`ngvm_dcache.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/ngvm_dcache.sv#L141-L174)): A's combinational ALU output (`dc_addr = a_addr`) drives the M10K read address (`rd_a`) at the `A -> M` clock edge. In `M` (`cmp`), tag comparison (`hit0 || hit1`) and byte/halfword rotation (`m_wa0`, `m_wa1`) happen combinationally ([`ngvm_dcache.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/ngvm_dcache.sv#L446-L451)), and the selected word is registered into `w_ra` at the `M -> W` clock edge ([`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L1235)). A consumer 2 clocks behind the load (stalled 1 cycle in `S` by `load_in_a`, [`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L1016-L1021)) is in `A` while the load is in `W`, and muxes `w_ra` via `afwd` (`L_WRA`, [`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L694-L702)).
* **ARM9v2** ([`arm9v2_core.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_core.sv#L517-L520), [`zap_dcache.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/zap/zap_dcache.sv#L208-L215)): A's combinational `a_data_address` drives `o_data_wb_adr_nxt` into the D-cache M10K read port at the `A -> M` edge. In `M`, tag match and `formatted` alignment happen combinationally ([`zap_dcache.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/zap/zap_dcache.sv#L431-L454)) and register into `tr_cache_words[0]` (`i_data_short_data`) at the `M -> W` edge. In cycle 3, the load is in `W` (`mw_value[1]`, [`arm9v2_core.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_core.sv#L421-L422)) and the late consumer in `A` selects `i_mw_value[1]` via `a.late` ([`arm9v2_execute.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_execute.sv#L197-L204)).
* Note also: 88.7 MHz in Pocket is a `11.27 ns` period (`12.50 ns` at its 80 MHz shipping clock). ARM9v2's `11.80 ns` path + `0.61 ns` skew (`12.41 ns` effective period = 80.6 MHz) is within `0.5 ns` of Pocket's raw delay—despite carrying an MMU/TLB, CP15, HUM lock scoreboard, and shared Wishbone cache that Pocket lacks.

#### B. Which Specific Structures Do Not Exist in Pocket (and Why S8/S9 Lost 1.6 ns vs. S6)

     1. Pocket has **no `lock_ff`**, **no HUM background load return**, **no `RELOAD0/1` / `HOLD` / `skid` machine**, and **no `wb_complete`**. In Pocket, tag comparators run only once at `D2 -> S` (`s_tag_next`, [`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L997-L1006)) and latch 6 boolean match bits into `s_ta`, `s_tb`, `s_tc`. And if `A` holds (`!a_free`), Pocket latches `a_a <= ea` and clears `a_la <= L_NONE` ([`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L1100-L1104)).
     3. By contrast, ARM9v2's [`arm9v2_execute.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_execute.sv#L341-L342) and [`arm9v2_execute.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_execute.sv#L386-L389) recompute a 16-bit `a.late` vector (`4 src * 2 prod * 2 lane`) through **fresh 6-bit tag comparators** against the combinational `i_next_tag` mux (which waits on `wb_complete`) on *both* the `A`-hold path (line 342) and the `S -> A` advance path (line 388). #### C. Why the Routing Share Is 65–73%
1. **Hard-block anchoring across quadrants**: The design uses 71 M10Ks and 3 DSPs across 16,561 ALMs. The 32 D-cache data-lane M10Ks (`g_way[0..1].u_zap_ram_simple_data_ram`), tag RAMs, and TLB M10Ks are physically pinned to M10K columns (`X42_Y11..Y22`), while `arm9v2_execute`'s ALU/shifter/multiplier and 500-bit stage records (`m`, `w`, `skid`) span adjacent LAB columns (`X35..X55`).
2. **High-fanout broadcast nets**:
   * `wb_complete~6` has **fanout 163** ([`2026-10-24-codex-arm9v2-s9.md`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/docs/consults/2026-10-24-codex-arm9v2-s9.md#L228)), driving `done`, pipeline enables on 500-bit `m`/`w`/`skid` registers, `w_load_select`, `wb_valid`, and `forward_tag` all at once. A single LUT driving 163 loads across 20+ LAB columns incurs `2.0–2.4 ns` of pure RC interconnect delay.
   * `o_data_wb_adr_nxt[11:4]` (`rd_set`, computed at the top of `A`'s ALU adder + `lookup_address` mux + `fcse`) fans out to **all 32 D-cache byte-lane M10Ks**, tag M10Ks, dirty/valid/LRU arrays, AND the 32 `g_short.forward_write` comparators.
   * `S9 Cut 4` (`RF_ALLOW_RETIMING = 0`, [`arm9v2_core.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_core.sv#L16)) saved 1,816 ALMs by stopping Quartus from retiming into `arm9v2_rf`, which cost `0.476 ns` hot (`-2.395 -> -2.871 ns`) because `u_execute` and `u_decode` were pulled physically closer to the un-retimed register file and further from `zap_dcache`.

### 2. Ranked Structural & Physical Options

Because all five families sit between **11.56 ns and 11.80 ns** (`-2.56` to `-2.87 ns` slack), **fixing only the top family improves worst slack by at most ~0.21 ns** before Family 2/3/4 takes the lead. Evaluate each option both by the family it removes and the cumulative ceiling.

#### Rank 1: Precompute Shift-Ready Match Vectors (`s_to_a_late` & `a_hold_late`) and Split `wb_complete` by Consumer Class
* *(Combines and sharpens Thread Proposal 1 + Fanout-163 Restructuring + Lock-Reduction Precomputation)*
* **Register boundary to add/move**:
  1. **Eliminate live 6-bit tag comparators on the `wb_complete` cone**: At `S -> A` ([`arm9v2_execute.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_execute.sv#L386-L389)) and on `A` hold ([`arm9v2_execute.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_execute.sv#L341-L342)), stop running `tag == i_next_tag` after `done`/`wb_complete`. There are only **two** possible next-producer tag states on any clock edge: **Case `advance` (`done=1`)** where `(next_M, next_W) = (a_or_skid.dest, m.dest)` and **Case `hold` (`done=0`)** where `(next_M, next_W) = (m.dest, w.dest)`.
     * Both tag pairs come directly from **already-registered stage flops** (`a`, `skid`, `m`, `w`)!
     * Compute the 6-bit tag equalities against `(a/skid.dest, m.dest)` and `(m.dest, w.dest)` **in parallel with `wb_complete`** (only 2 LUT levels from `m.dest`/`w.dest`/`a.dest` flops), and use a dedicated copy of `done` (`done_exec`, `/* synthesis preserve */` or `maxfan = 32`) only as the **final 2:1 mux select** right at the D-input of `a.late` (`a[52:41]`) and `o_forward_match` ([`arm9v2_decode.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_decode.sv#L440-L444)).
     * Better yet: for an instruction already in `S`, its match against `M` (which becomes `W` on `done=1`) is **already registered in `s.match[src][0][lane]`** (`m` producer)! Just like Pocket's `late_of(s_ta)`, `a.late`'s `W`-producer half when `done=1` does not even need a tag comparator—it is `s.match[src][0][lane]` gated with `m_stays_live` (masked only if `m` is a newly-locking miss)!
  2. **Move `w_locked` and `any_dc_lock` reduction to the `lock_ff` installation edge**: In [`zap_dcache_fsm.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/zap/zap_dcache_fsm.sv#L405-L427) / [`arm9v2_core.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_core.sv#L332), maintain registered `any_dc_lock_q` (1 bit) and precompute `m_locked_q = |(lock_nxt & m_load_select)` at the `M -> W` edge so `w_locked` in `W` is a **1-LUT combine** (`w_locked_q || (lock_return_valid && ...)`), removing the 64-bit `WideOr0` tree (`~1.9 ns`) from `wb_complete`.
  3. **Split `wb_complete` (`fanout 163`) into 3 explicit logic-duplicated wires** (`/* synthesis keep */`): `wb_complete_fwd` (driving only `a.late` / `s.match` 2:1 muxes, fanout ~32), `wb_complete_pipe` (driving `m`/`w`/`skid` stage enables), and `wb_complete_retire` (driving retirement/return).
* **Expected gain**: **Removes Family 1 (`11.803 ns -> ~8.9–9.2 ns`, ~2.6–2.9 ns reduction on that cone)**. Net chip slack gain by itself: **+0.21 ns** (`-2.871 -> -2.658 ns`), because **Family 4 (`11.618 ns`) and Family 2 (`11.588 ns`) immediately take the lead**.
* **Cycle cost**: **0 cycles.** All 3/4/5 hit windows, miss replay, and firmware boot cycles are bit-identical.
* **Risk**: Low (pure boolean factoring: `eq(tag, done ? tagA : tagB) === done ? eq(tag, tagA) : eq(tag, tagB)`).
* **Effort**: Small (~60 lines in `arm9v2_core.sv`, `arm9v2_execute.sv`, `arm9v2_decode.sv`).

#### Rank 2: Register the D-Cache / D-TLB Request & Check Owner (`check_record_q` / `w_store_owner_q`)

#### Rank 5 (Sceptical / Fallback Option): 3-Clock Late Pickup Instead of 2-Clock (Registering Late Match / Bypass 1 Stage Earlier)
* **Direct answer to the brief's question**: *Is a 3-clock late pickup the Pocket's actual schedule relative to its cache, or is ours already equivalent?*
  * **Ours is already equivalent to Pocket's.** Both ARM9v2 and Pocket have a **2-clock (1-stall) late pickup** (`raw hit window = 4 clocks` from load `S` to dependent consumer `M/W`) because both launch the M10K read at `A -> M`, align/tag-match in `M`, register the hit word at `M -> W`, and mux that registered `W` word at `A`'s ALU input (`arm9v2_execute.sv:201` `a.late` == `arm_cpu.sv:1032` `afwd` `L_WRA`).
  * Changing ARM9v2 to a **3-clock late pickup** (2 stalls on unshifted load-use, `raw hit window = 5 clocks`, matching early operands) means the load is already retired (`W -> RF`) before the consumer reaches `A`, so **the `W -> A` late load bypass disappears** (or `a.late` is selected at `D2 -> S` and muxed into `a.left`/`a.right` at the `S -> A` edge *before* stage `A`).
* **Register boundary moved**: Move `left_a` / `right_a` / `third_a` muxing ([`arm9v2_execute.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_execute.sv#L201-L204)) from **after** the `a` stage register to **before** the `a` stage register (so `a.left`, `a.right`, `a.third` are registered ALU inputs with zero bypass mux in `A`), OR keep `M -> A` ALU late bypass (1-clock ALU-to-ALU, where the producer is in `D2/S` when the consumer is in `D1/D2`) and drop only the **2-clock `W -> A` load late pickup**.
  * Wait—distinguish those two sub-options carefully!
    * **Sub-option 5a (Drop only 2-clock `W -> A` load late pickup; keep `a.late` for ALU producers)**: Does **not** remove the `a.late` mux before `u_alu` (Family 5 stays!) and only simplifies `w_load_select` slightly. **Bad trade.**
    * **Sub-option 5b (Move the entire late bypass mux before the `a` register—i.e., remove late bypass at `A` input)**: Eliminates Family 1 (`a.late`) AND cuts 2 LUT levels (~0.8 ns) off Family 5 (`A` ALU -> `o_data_wb_adr_nxt`), worth **~0.8–1.0 ns** on Family 1 & Family 5, **but** every unshifted load-use pays +1 stall (`3/4/5 -> 3/5/5` raw hit windows) AND `S`-stage early producers (`shifts/multiplies`) can no longer forward in 1 clock to `A` unless `S` stalls!
* **Cycle cost**: Raw hit windows degrade from **`3 / 4 / 5` to `3 / 5 / 5`** (every unshifted load-use `LDR r0, [r1]; ADD r2, r0, #1` or pointer chase `LDR r0, [r0]` pays **2 stall cycles instead of 1**, costing **~4–6% of the 13% EKA1 boot speedup**).
* **Risk**: Medium (invalidates the 4-clock late-load timing window tests in the 85-window suite).
* **Effort**: Medium.

### 3. The Honest Ceiling at 104 MHz and the Best Fallback if Needed

#### Where the Zero-Cycle-Loss Design Plausibly Lands
* Today's five critical families (`11.56–11.80 ns`) are **not** irreducible ARM9 execute paths—they are artifacts of (1) evaluating 6-bit tag comparators *after* `wb_complete` instead of muxing pre-compared `advance`/`hold` matches with `done`, (2) muxing `check_record` + `fcse` combinationally before the D-TLB, and (3) comparing `A`'s live ALU output against `W`'s store set index *before* the `A -> M` clock edge inside `SHORT_READ`.
* With **Ranks 1 + 2 + 3 + 4** applied (zero cycle cost), the `cpu_trial` worst-corner setup slack plausibly lands at **`-0.75 ns` to `-1.10 ns`** (within `0.2–0.5 ns` of the shipping ZAP core's `-0.584 ns` at identical ~16.6k ALM area).
* **Whole-device reality check**: In the full `NGage` core (which adds `0.3–1.0 ns` of congestion penalty), `-0.75` to `-1.10 ns` in `cpu_trial` translates to **`-1.1` to `-1.8 ns` full-chip slow-100C slack** (~90–93 MHz static worst-case model, which typically runs reliably on physical Cyclone V silicon at room temperature for `slack >= -1.2 ns`, though tight if it lands at `-1.8 ns`).

#### If It Cannot Reach `~ -1.0 ns` Reliable Board Margin: Which Fallback Wins?
Do **not** drop the 2-clock late bypass first, and do **not** add a 9th pipeline stage to `F/D/S`. Here is the strict hierarchy of fallbacks by **ns gained per % of EKA1 performance lost**:

1. **Best Fallback (if `-0.4 ns` more is needed): Drop Same-Cycle Background Miss Retention (`lock_ff` Scoreboard / HUM) -> Keep Hit-Under-Miss Blocking or Simple 1-Entry Lock**
   * **What changes**: Replace the 64-bit `lock_ff` bitmask ([`zap_dcache_fsm.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/zap/zap_dcache_fsm.sv#L405)) + `return_select` + `held` lock-retention machinery with either a **single 4-bit locked register ID** (`lock_reg_id_q[3:0]` + `lock_valid_q`, which tracks 1 outstanding load—95%+ of ARM9 code has at most 1 non-blocking load in flight!) or stall on D-cache miss like Pocket.
   * **Gain**: Saves **~800–1,100 ALMs** and removes the 64-bit `lock_ff` / `return_select` / `w_load_select` trees completely (**+0.35 to +0.50 ns** from area decompression and shorter routing).
   * **Cycle cost**: With a **1-entry lock tag (`4-bit ==` instead of 64-bit bitmap)**, single-load HUM is **100% preserved** (3/4/5 hit windows untouched; EKA1 boot impact **< 0.5%**; only back-to-back dual misses overlap differently). Even dropping HUM completely costs only **~1.5–2.5%** on EKA1 boot while preserving all `3/4/5` hit windows!
2. **Second Fallback (if `A` ALU -> D-cache `ADDR_A` is still the ceiling): Split `A` / `M` Memory Address Generation vs. Cache Lookup only on Unaligned/Late-Shifted, OR Add `M2` Cache Stage? No—Use Pre-Computed Base+Imm12 in `S` for Common Loads**
   * Adding a stage between `A` and `M` (`A -> M1 -> M2 -> W`) turns the 2-clock late pickup into a 3-clock pickup anyway (`3/5/6` windows).
   * Dropping the **2-clock late load pickup** (`3/4/5 -> 3/5/5` windows) costs **4–6% of IPC** on pointer-heavy Symbian EKA1 code. Only touch the 2-clock late load pickup if `a.late -> u_alu -> M10K ADDR_A` remains below `-1.3 ns` *after* Rank 3 removes the `SHORT_READ` comparator from `A`'s ALU path.
