## Milestone 1: architectural writer masks

D2 and its held S-entry carry two real destination writer bits. Every
pending-load and cache-lock destination comparison is qualified by its
writer bit. DP tests, stores without base update and branches without link
have no GPR destination; ordinary ALU instructions do not write the unused
Rn lane. Loads, SWP, MRC (except PC/flags), long multiply and base writeback
retain their actual destinations. SPSR MSR uses its banked SPSR destination.
Block loads write each transfer lane, with base writeback only on the last
transfer and the existing base-in-load-list suppression. Fetch faults have
no writers. Conditions are still conservatively checked later.

The consult's claim that the unused lane always defaults to r0 is too
broad: it normally contains decoded Rn. MOV's unused Rn field commonly
encodes r0; the unqualified comparison is the actual defect.

ZAP and both V2 modes pass **264/264 and 68 QEMU comparisons**. Every ZAP
cycle/instruction pair equals the merged master reference. Both lint modes
and generation checks pass. Source/generated decode benches pass 6,586,941
checks each, including every nonempty block list's writer lanes.
Only seven existing window expectations change, all explained by removed
false hazards: independent load raw 7->3, LDM1 7->2, LDM3 7->4, the three
load-distance windows 9/10/11->8, and no-register branch 8->3 (off 15->10).
Dependent LDR/ADD stays raw 8. No binary or QEMU reference is rebuilt.

## Milestone 2: registered source classification and match ownership

D2/S now carries early/late source classification. Shifted Rm and register
shift counts are early; multiply factors are early, while its accumulator
operands are late. Bases, unshifted Rm, store data and PSR reads are late.
This commit records the classification without yet relaxing its interlocks.

The sole S forwarding selector consumes registered match bits, not source
or producer tags. D2 compares against the producer tags/write masks that
will occupy the registers after the edge. During a held S slot it recomputes
the matches for that same owner each edge, while execute retains resolved
values as producers leave. A bench assertion compares those look-ahead bits
with the producer/consumer records actually present, covering normal advance,
hold, reload, retry and flush. Cache ACK does not qualify D2 admission.

This intermediate milestone retains the existing three output records and
late token so every cycle remains identical to milestone 1. The next change
folds the duplicate registered execute output into the core's A/M record;
S then selects only M/W, and late operands get the small A input bypass.
Without that fold, an extra output-to-M transfer would still prevent the
requested two-clock late-consumer spacing. The D-TLB check remains sourced
from a registered M/W address, never the combinational ALU result.

All three configurations pass **264/264 and 68 references**, and every
cycle/instruction pair equals milestone 1. Source/generated execute benches
pass 68,913 checks each; decode passes 6,586,943. Joined I-cache/S/A tests
retain 270/401/677 clocks at waits 0/3/9 in source and generated forms.
The harness needed its look-ahead assignments moved below the packed-array
declarations: sv2v otherwise treated forward-referenced array slices as
scalar bits. Both emu lint modes and generation check pass.
[Validation](arm9v2/s7-match-validation.json). The integrated fit still follows
the completed cycle gate, as requested; no timing improvement is claimed yet.

All 85 timing windows at all three latencies are identical to milestone 1
in both predictor modes.

## Milestone 3: two-producer S and late A bypass

S selects only registered M/W words using its registered matches. A's late
operands select M/W with matches captured at S/A and refreshed while held;
held resolved values survive producer departure. Early operands retain the
D2 interlock. The redundant execute-output register is folded into the
core's A/M record: A launches the cache RAM lookup, M's registered address
still drives the D-TLB check, and W owns the response. Redirect/training
remain registered events with separate PC/state/checkpoint metadata, so
folding the data record cannot train or restore a different instruction.
The first block-transfer base is captured after its late bypass.

The multiplier factors remain registered before the DSP products. Its
accumulator is late through the final add, including when a backend hold
returns the older producer after the private product clocks. An initial
candidate captured that accumulator too soon: rand4 diverged after SMLAL
at PC 0x1b64 (instruction 2389). The fixed design agrees; an additional
standalone held-accumulator test covers this case.

Load admission is deliberately still conservative in this commit. Its
folded path changes dependent LDR raw 8->7, while independent raw 3 and
predicted branch raw 2 remain unchanged. The next commit installs replay
before enabling speculative scalar-load admission. Serial/exception windows
are retargeted to their exact last two visits; cache-line phase changes are
recorded, not hidden behind tolerances. No instruction stream was rebuilt.

All three full configurations pass **264/264 and 68 QEMU comparisons**;
ZAP pairs are unchanged. All 85 windows pass at each latency. Source and
generated leaf benches pass 885,056 checks, execute 68,916, decode 6,586,943;
joined S/A/cache completes in 270/401/676 clocks. Both lint modes and
cpu-gen-check pass. [All counts](arm9v2/s7-split-validation.json).

## Milestone 4: scalar hit pickup and registered retirement replay

The wrapper exposes the existing registered short-port word and its
registered tag/permission predicates before the FSM's response mux. No
cache RAM, FSM, bus scheduling or ZAP behavior changes. W carries a bounded
load-writer bit. Its speculative producer word is zero unless the registered
hit and permission checks qualify it; no ACK enters the operand selector or
D2/S admission. The background completion still writes the register file;
its registered lock prevents a replayed consumer from issuing prematurely.

Scalar loads to ordinary registers release late consumers after one hold,
and early consumers after two. WAW hazards for those scalar loads may also
speculate: in-order retirement plus recovery protects them from a delayed
background write. LDM, SWP and PC loads retain their conservative protection.
Every scalar load that misses its first qualified hit, retries, waits, or
passes through fill-installation recovery requests a registered retirement
flush to its next PC. The load retires once; only younger work is replayed.
Data abort and other exception vectors take priority. Partial LDM never
uses this restart-at-next-instruction mechanism. W's one-hot destination
selector travels with W through holds and reloads, replacing indexed lock
and background-return selection.

A backend hold can compress the load/consumer distance even after the D2
hazard check: an A load and a consumer waiting in S could advance together
on release. The initial candidate missed this and corrupted the cached
random tests' initialization copy (first crand1 divergence after PC 0x3f4).
A registered S-entry load wait now compares the next A/M load owners at the
same boundary as the forwarding matches, refreshes through holds and
preserves the two/three-clock separation. It does not inspect cache ACK.

The warmed raw marker intervals are independent **3**, dependent unshifted
**4**, dependent shifted **5**: subtract the two surrounding marker slots to
obtain the requested **1 / 2 / 3** instruction spacings. Register-specified
shift amount is raw 6, including its architectural extra shift clock.
Load base/store data and byte/halfword/signed/unaligned consumers are raw 4.
Predicted B/BL/BX stay raw 2. The small-LDM tail remains conservatively held;
its dependent-block window is 10, down from the session-start 11.

All configurations pass **264/264 and 68 QEMU comparisons**, including
cache, dhold_rtl, dhum_rtl and dstore_rtl at all latencies and both predictor
settings. ZAP's 264 cycle/instruction pairs remain exactly the master
reference. All 85 windows pass at each latency. The bench also checks the
W lock selector's owner, the qualified forwarded word against the retired
word, forwarding-match ownership and recovery suppressing younger requests
and retirement. Both lint modes and generation checks pass.
[All counts](arm9v2/s7-replay-validation.json).

The hit-load proof succeeds; the whole-test gain does **not** yet meet the
cycle gate. Replay/refill costs offset it. RAS repair is the final requested
functional milestone before applying the stop decision. No timing fit has
been started or claimed for this source.
