## Cache milestone

The short D-port now updates replacement state from the accepted response's
registered way and address, including hits under a fill. Replayed, faulting
and unaccepted younger probes cannot update it. A same-set accepted hit is
also forwarded into the following lookup's victim choice; otherwise that
lookup would use LRU captured before the preceding hit was acknowledged.
The tag compare and hit-data word selection are unchanged. Only victim
metadata sees the additional replacement bypass.

The feature is a constant parameter on the shared tag manager, enabled only
by the short D-port. ZAP and the I-cache retain their previous update path.
The D-cache FSM is untouched. This keeps the eventual cherry-pick independent
of the parked FSM changes. Pending hit updates retain their existing priority
over the older fill/store metadata; same-edge installation and HUM cannot
both acknowledge a CPU hit under the existing FSM contract.

Testing distinguished two necessary cases. Accepting all updates without
the replacement bypass failed `arm9_cache_lru_rtl` check 2, at 55,405 clocks
at 0:0. Preserving speculative idle updates while adding only HUM updates
still failed `dhum_rtl` check 26: a younger B probe changed replacement state
while older miss C was replayed. The implemented path fixes both, rather
than weakening either test.

Full regressions pass **261/261** in ZAP, V2 predictor-on and V2 predictor-off,
with **68 QEMU agreements and zero differences** in each. Every ZAP cycle and
instruction count equals session 4. Existing binaries and QEMU references
were reused. All cache tests, including `dhum_rtl`, `dhold_rtl`, `dcwf`,
`dcwf_rtl`, `cache`, `cache_rtl`, `dcache_rtl`, `icache_rtl`,
`icache_restart`, `arm9_cache_edge`, `arm9_cache_lru_rtl` and `walloc_rtl`,
pass at 0:0 / 0:3 / 2:9 in both predictor settings. Existing timing-window
counts pass without retargeting. Full counts are retained in

## Structural write-through pass

The same-edge RF write-through has been split at D2/S: the raw RF words,
three write words, and twelve match bits are captured independently with
exactly the old payload capacity enable. Selection now uses only those
registers. Priority remains background C > B > A, followed by S's existing
three younger A/M/W producers. It adds no stage, changes no owner or hazard,
and does not bring cache ACK into admission or forwarding. The registers
carry `dont_retime` so fitting cannot reconstruct the old retirement-to-word
mux path across the boundary. The risk is moving the small write-through
word mux onto the S datapath; the integrated fit below measures the resulting
path families.

All three configurations again pass **261/261 runs, 68 QEMU agreements**.
Every cycle and instruction count, including all V2 counts, equals the cache
milestone. The 85 existing windows pass without edits; additional measurement
runs retain both final visits at all three latencies in
[on](arm9v2/s5-write-through-windows-on.json) /
[off](arm9v2/s5-write-through-windows-off.json). The source/generated decode
benches include same-edge three-port priority and held-owner tests; decode
and S/A with the real I-cache pass at waits 0/3/9 with unchanged counts.
Both emu lint modes and `cpu-gen-check` pass. The full regression manifest is
[here](arm9v2/s5-write-through-validation.json).

The structural pass fits at **-2.109 ns at 100C / -1.665 ns at -40C**,
with **15,745 ALMs**. Compared with the cache-only candidate, worst-slow
improves 0.440 ns and area falls 97 ALMs. Compared with session-4 seed 2,
worst-slow is 0.150 ns worse. All four hold corners are positive (minimum
+0.129 ns). Against the existing same-cache seed-2 ZAP figure of 16,549 ALMs,
this is 804 ALMs / 4.9% smaller. No new ZAP fit was needed: its synthesized
configuration is unchanged. The fitted RTL and source hashes are retained in
[the structural-pass manifest](arm9v2/s5-write-through-seed2/manifest.json).

**The -2 ns structural stopping rule is reached after this pass.** It is
0.109 ns below that threshold at 100C. This is a seed-2 result, not a
three-seed median. Seeds 3/4 of the final integrated cycle-model milestone
were not started because that milestone has not been reached. No additional
path tuning, late-pickup implementation or RAS change follows this result.

## Exact stopping point and continuation

The minimal shared-cache handoff fix is isolated in **`c85eacc`**; its FSM
is untouched, and all 261 ZAP cycle/instruction pairs remain the reference.
The subsequent registered write-through cut is retained because it preserves
every measured cycle and improves worst slack and area. ZAP remains default.
All session-5 RTL is generated and tested. No `sys/` change, firmware boot,
whole-device build or board measurement was performed. The shared Quartus
image was reused; both session containers exited with `--rm`, and none of
this task's Quartus containers remains running.

Session-5 items 2 and 3 are **not implemented**: the real three-clock S load
pickup, true-writer masks, miss/hold replay and W RAS checkpoint repair still
need work. Item 4's current full-suite/QEMU/window/all-row comparison is
recorded, but it is not acceptance of the requested new cycle model. The
three-seed final integration fit and whole-device build are not reached.
The phone-speed goal is not demonstrated by these CPU-only results.

The next session first needs a structural decision on the three families
above, under the lead's stop rule. The bounded S producer qualifiers can
be registered with their A/M/W payloads; the selected memory check address
and mode must arrive at the D-TLB from a register instead of the recovery
state/FCSE mux. Those are proposals, not measured fixes. Keep the multiplier's
registered inputs and one forwarding network.

After the timing guardrail is cleared, implement actual destination masks
and release a scalar-load dependent into S when its producer reaches M;
forward the cache's registered, permission-qualified short-hit word from W.
Do not forward the FSM's general data mux or use ACK to qualify admission.
An early miss ACK installs its lock on the same edge the dependent sees the
old lock: simply deleting M/W scoreboard entries is incorrect. A registered
retirement replay can discard younger speculative operands after a missed,
retried or held scalar load, with abort priority preserved. A partial LDM
cannot replay to the next architectural PC without losing remaining
transfers; retain its protection or carry an explicit micro-op replay
checkpoint. Register the normal/background return ownership and prove the
hit, miss, uncached, fill-installation, abort and held-backend cases before
retargeting the windows. Restore W's pre-instruction RAS checkpoint on
recovery (applying its call/return action once); predictor invalidation must
not silently override an intentional checkpoint repair.
