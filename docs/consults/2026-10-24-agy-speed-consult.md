## Executive Verdict: Two First-Order Misdiagnoses to Correct Immediately

Before ranking the options, two facts in the RTL overturn common assumptions in the brief:

1. **The 48% `code` stall counter is NOT a 48% I-cache miss/fill stall — it is ~42% `fifo_full` (backend stalls) and only ~5–6% I-cache miss/fill.**
   In [`zap_core.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer/rtl/cpu/zap/zap_core.sv#L664-L665), `code_stall` is defined as:
   ```systemverilog
   assign code_stall = fifo_full |
                      (o_instr_wb_stb & o_instr_wb_cyc & ~i_instr_wb_ack);
   ```
   While [`tools/mister_dbg.py`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer/tools/mister_dbg.py#L86-L87) labels `code_stall` ([`zap_core.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer/rtl/cpu/zap/zap_core.sv#L1775-L1776)) as *"I-cache not ready"*, `code_stall` asserts whenever the 4-entry [`zap_fifo`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/zap/zap_fifo.sv#L119-L133) is **full** because the **backend** is stalled (`stall_from_issue` 35%, `stall_from_shifter` 17%, `data_stall` 10%, `stall_from_decode` 7%, or multi-uop LDM/STM expansion in `predecode`) while the I-cache hits.
   We can prove the true I-cache miss rate today from beat conservation in the `b22` Sonic N counters ([`docs/PROGRESS-LOG.md`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer/docs/PROGRESS-LOG.md#L4024-L4032)):
   - Total CPU Wishbone beats (`dbg_bus_beat`, [`NGage.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer/NGage.sv#L1043)): **5.66 M beats/s**.
   - D-cache beats (`cpu_dbg_dcache`, [`zap_dcache_fsm.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/zap/zap_dcache_fsm.sv#L374-L387)):
     $$4 \times (311\text{ K}_{\text{ld-fill}} + 332\text{ K}_{\text{st-fill}} + 300\text{ K}_{\text{wb}}) + 1 \times 301\text{ K}_{\text{unc}} = \mathbf{4.073\text{ M beats/s}}$$
   - Remaining bus beats for **all** I-cache fills + I/D-TLB walks:
     $$5.660\text{ M} - 4.073\text{ M} = \mathbf{1.587\text{ M beats/s}} \implies \mathbf{\le 397\text{ K I-cache fills/s}}$$
   At 13–15 clocks/fill (~1.3% misses/instruction at 30.2 M instr/s), **I-cache fills consume only 5.0–5.8 M clocks/s (4.8%–5.6% of 104 MHz)**. The real bottleneck is **CPU pipeline CPI (2.6–2.7 CPI before memory)** plus **D-side memory stalls (~10% of clocks)**.

2. **`arm9v2` is currently at ZAP's CPI in random game proxies not just because of missing 3-cycle load-use, but because of an unmasked WAW load-hazard bug in [`arm9v2_decode.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_decode.sv#L420-L431), a 6-stage `load_valid` lock window, and a full RAS wipe on every `W` flush in [`arm9v2_core.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_core.sv#L612-L614).**
   Even more importantly: because `arm9v2` kept ZAP's shared `zap_dcache` pipeline (where `tr_cache_words` is registered at the end of `M` in [`zap_dcache.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/zap/zap_dcache.sv#L417-L435) rather than at the `M->W` CPU register with a late `A`-stage bypass), `arm9v2`'s *planned* best-case hit load-use penalty is **2 stall cycles (3 clocks total)** for *all* dependent instructions, whereas the Analogue Pocket core ([`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer/../../../../../private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L708-L791)) pays **1 stall cycle** for unshifted ALU/address operands (the majority of load consumers) and **2 stall cycles** only for shifted/multiplied operands.

### Detailed Breakdown of the Six Architectural Differences

#### 1. The Load-Use Schedule (Why Pocket pays 1–2 stalls and `arm9v2` pays 5 stalls today / 2 planned)
- **How Pocket (`arm_cpu.sv`) does it**:
  At the `A -> M` clock edge, [`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L1045) drives `dc_addr = a_addr` directly into the D-cache M10K address ports (`rd_a = dc_addr`, [`ngvm_dcache.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/ngvm_dcache.sv#L148)). During `M`, the M10Ks output both ways' raw 32-bit words (`dc_rdata`, `dc_rdata1`) and tag match (`dc_rsel`, [`ngvm_dcache.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/ngvm_dcache.sv#L317-L319)). In parallel during `M`, [`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L1160-L1161) byte-rotates/sign-extends both ways (`m_wa0 = align(dc_rdata)`, `m_wa1 = align(dc_rdata1)`) and registers the selected word into `w_ra` at the `M -> W` edge ([`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L1235)).
  Crucially, Pocket splits operand consumers into **early** (needed in `S`: shifter inputs `rb`/`rc`, multiplier operands) and **late** (`s_blate`/`s_clate` and `ra`, needed only at the start of `A`, [`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L540-L545)):
  - If the instruction after `LDR` uses the loaded register as `Rn` (`ra`), an unshifted `Rm` (`LSL #0`, `s_blate`), or a store's `Rd` (`s_clate`), [`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L735-L746) stalls it in `S` for **only 1 clock** (while `LDR` is in `A`), lets it advance to `A` while `LDR` is in `M`, and at the `A` input muxes `w_ra` via `afwd` (`L_WRA`, [`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L778))!
  - Only if the consumer shifts or multiplies the loaded register does it stall **2 clocks** in `S` and pick up `w_ra` via `sfwd` ([`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L678)).
- **Why `arm9v2` is much slower today (and 1 clock slower even when Session 6 finishes)**:
  1. **Current 8-clock dependent `LDR` (`+5` stall clocks)**: In [`arm9v2_decode.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_decode.sv#L420-L431), `load_hazard` holds any dependent instruction in `D2` while the load is in `d_dest`, `a_a_dest`, `a_dest`, `m.dest`, `w.dest`, or `skid.dest` (`load_valid[5:0]` in [`arm9v2_core.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_core.sv#L365-L375)). The consumer cannot even enter `S` until the load has exited `W`!
  2. **Current false WAW stall on *independent* instructions (`ldr_independent` = 7 clocks)**: Look at [`arm9v2_decode.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_decode.sv#L424-L425):
     ```systemverilog
     for (int dst = 0; dst < 2; dst++) begin
         if (d2.dest[dst] == i_load_tag[pending]) load_hazard = 1'b1;
     ```
     `d2` does not carry `writes[1:0]`! When `d2` is an instruction that only writes 1 register (or 0 registers, like `CMP`/`B`/`STR`), its unused `d2.dest[1]` defaults to `6'd0` (`r0`, [`arm9v2_decode.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_decode.sv#L121)). Thus, whenever a load to **`r0`** is in flight anywhere in the pipeline, **every single instruction in `D2` stalls for 5 clocks** even if it doesn't read or write `r0`! Since `r0` is the C ABI return/first-argument scratch register, this constantly stalls game code.
  3. **Structural limit of `zap_dcache` (`SHORT_READ=1`)**: In [`zap_dcache.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/zap/zap_dcache.sv#L417-L435), `SHORT_READ` reads the RAM on `i_address_nxt` (`A -> M` edge), then registers `tr_cache_words` at the **`M -> W` edge**, and only formats/outputs `o_dat` in `W` ([`arm9v2_core.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_core.sv#L540-L545)). Because `o_dat` is a combinational output of `W` (rather than already sitting in a clean `w_ra` CPU flip-flop at the start of `W`), forwarding `o_dat` into `S` in `W` requires **2 stall cycles** for *all* load consumers (3 clocks total), and injects the D-cache word mux directly into the `S` shifter path unless `A`-stage late forwarding is added for unshifted ALU/base operands.

#### 2. The Forwarding Network & `S`-Stage Critical Path (-2.109 ns)
- **How Pocket (`arm_cpu.sv`) closes 96 MHz**:
  In `S`, Pocket **never** forwards from `A`! Think about why: an instruction in `S` cannot possibly use the ALU result of the instruction currently in `A` *during `S`* because the ALU in `A` hasn't computed it yet!
  - If the instruction in `S` needs the result of the instruction in `A` **unshifted** (in `A`), it doesn't need it in `S` at all — it advances to `A` when the producer advances to `M`, and picks up `m_ra`/`m_rb` at the start of `A` via the tiny 3-input `afwd` mux ([`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L774-L791))!
  - If the instruction in `S` needs the result of the instruction in `A` **shifted** (in `S`), it **must** stall 1 clock in `S` (`s_hold` on `a_wa`/`a_wb`, [`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L737-L742)), at which point the producer is in `M` (`m_ra`/`m_rb`)!
  - Consequently, Pocket's `S`-stage forwarding mux (`sfwd`, [`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L673-L681)) **only has 4 inputs (`m_ra`, `m_rb`, `w_ra`, `w_rb`)**, driven by **precomputed match flops** (`s_ta..s_td`, computed in `D2` in [`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L991-L1018))!
- **Why `arm9v2` is at -2.109 ns in `S -> A` ([`s5 report`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/docs/consults/2026-10-23-codex-arm9v2-s5.md#L165-L216))**:
  `arm9v2` does **all** operand forwarding in `S` ([`arm9v2_execute.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_execute.sv#L78-L96)) by holding the producer's output in an extra register bank `a.dest_data` (which is actually the `A->M` boundary register duplicated inside `u_execute`, while `m` is `M->W` and `w` is a post-`W` shadow register). Worse, [`arm9v2_forward.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_forward.sv#L17-L30) performs **combinational 6-bit tag comparisons across 6 producer lanes + PC/CPSR special-case muxes inside `S`**, and then feeds that directly into the barrel shifter (`arm9v2_rotate.sv`) for *all* operands, even unshifted ones! That single design choice accounts for the 12-level, -2.109 ns critical path (`m[506] -> u_execute.a[96]`).

#### 3. Fetch Queue, LDM/STM Decoder, and BTB/RAS
- **Fetch queue size & LDM decoding**:
  Pocket uses a 4-entry instruction buffer (`ib[0:3]`, [`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L261)) and precomputes the 16-bit LDM/STM popcount and first-register index **per buffer entry** (`g_ls`, [`arm_cpu.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_cpu.sv#L296-L327)) *before* the buffer read mux.
  `arm9v2` has an **8-entry** fetch queue (`queue[0:7]` in [`arm9v2_fetch.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_fetch.sv#L48), 96 bits × 8 = 768 flops + 8:1 read mux) **plus** a 2-entry `d1[0:1]` queue + `d2` + `issued` in [`arm9v2_decode.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_decode.sv#L31-L34) — 12 instruction slots of buffering in front of `S`!
- **The RAS wipe bug in `arm9v2`**:
  In [`arm9v2_core.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_core.sv#L612-L614):
  ```systemverilog
  wire [1:0] redirect_ras_action = wb_flush ? 2'd3 : ...
  wire [2:0] redirect_ras_ptr    = wb_flush ? 3'd0 : ...
  wire [3:0] redirect_ras_count  = wb_flush ? 4'd0 : ...
  ```
  Whenever `W` flushes the pipeline (e.g. an indirect call/jump via `LDR pc`, a mispredicted `LDM sp!, {.., pc}` return, or an IRQ/SWI return `MOVS pc, lr`), `redirect_ras_action = 2'd3` (`ras_count <= 0`) **wipes the entire 8-entry Return Address Stack**! Every caller up the C call stack subsequently mispredicts its return in `W` (costing 7 cycles each). Carrying the 7-bit `{ras_ptr, ras_count}` checkpoint down to `W` (already planned for Session 6) fixes this immediately.

#### 4. Register File (`arm_regs.sv` vs. `zap_register_file.sv`)
- Pocket's [`arm_regs.sv`](file:///private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-jacob-dev-fpga-mister-NGage-MiSTer/a3d3155e-0d5b-4fb6-bc9d-97caf4499fda/scratchpad/pocket-src/n-gage/rtl/arm_regs.sv#L1-L44) has 32 registers (1,024 flops), no reset logic (saving LAB control-set and LUT input terms), 5 read ports, and 2 write ports (`wa`, `wb`). ## Question 2: Ranked Paths to ~47 M Instructions/s in Play

To reach **47 M instructions/s** on the board, we must satisfy:
$$\text{Throughput} = \frac{f_{\text{CPU}}}{\text{CPI}_{\text{play}}} \ge 47\text{ M/s}$$
At $f_{\text{CPU}} = 104\text{ MHz}$, we need **$\text{CPI}_{\text{play}} \le 2.21$** (down from ZAP's **3.30** today — a **33.0% cycle reduction**, or **-1.09 CPI**).

### Rank 1 — Path (a) + Targeted Shared-Memory Improvements: Finish `arm9v2` with 1/2-Cycle Load-Use, `D2` Pre-Matched Forwarding, and D-Cache Write Buffer
- **Why it ranks #1**: It is the **only** path that can credibly reach 45–48 M instructions/s without breaking peripheral timing or rewriting the EKA1 MMU/CP15 contract from scratch.
- **Concrete CPI & Frequency Math**:
  - Starting from ZAP's **3.30 CPI** (31.5 M/s) at 104 MHz:
    1. **Fetch-cycle BTB + RAS (with `W` RAS checkpoint fix)**: ZAP has a 1-cycle-late BTB and flushes 390K/s from ALU + 150K/s from WB (~540K flushes/s × 7–8 clocks = ~4.0 M clocks/s, plus decode branch redirect bubbles). Moving to `arm9v2`'s fetch-cycle BTB + working 8-entry RAS saves **~0.28–0.35 CPI** (matching `ARM9V2.md`'s measured `call_ret` 0.703 ratio and Pocket's BTB delta).
    2. **Load-use schedule & HUM**: On Sonic N / Asphalt, loads are ~20% of instructions (~6–9 M loads/s). In ZAP, back-to-back dependent loads/ALU stall in `issue`/`shifter` (`stall_from_issue` is 35% of clocks!).
       - With Session 6's planned **2-stall-cycle (3-clock)** load-use + fixing the [`arm9v2_decode.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_decode.sv#L424-L425) `d2.writes` WAW bug: saves **~0.35–0.42 CPI** over ZAP.
       - Adding **Pocket-style `A`-stage late bypass (`afwd` from `W` load data)** so unshifted ALU and base-address consumers of a hit load stall **1 clock instead of 2**: at ~40% of loads feeding an immediate unshifted consumer, saves an additional **~0.12–0.16 CPI**.
    3. **Faster LDM/STM & Multiply**: `arm9v2` decodes 1 register/clock in `D2` without ZAP's predecode/issue bubbles and runs `MUL` in 2 cycles in `A` (`arm9v2_execute.sv:221`) vs ZAP's multi-cycle shifter/ALU stall: saves **~0.15–0.20 CPI**.
    4. **Shared memory-side levers (D-cache write buffer + I-cache wrap-4 CWF + 16 KB D-cache)**: saves **~0.18–0.25 CPI** (detailed in Question 3).
  - **Total Projected `arm9v2` CPI in Play**:
    $$3.30 - (0.32_{\text{branch}} + 0.48_{\text{load-use/issue}} + 0.17_{\text{LDM/MUL}} + 0.21_{\text{mem}}) = \mathbf{2.12\text{ CPI}}$$
  - **At 104 MHz**: $\frac{104\text{ MHz}}{2.12} = \mathbf{49.1\text{ M instructions/s}}$ (**44–49 M/s** range).
- **Realistic Timing Closure at 104 MHz**:
  Why did Pocket close at 96 MHz CPU-only and 80 MHz whole-core while `arm9v2` is at -2.109 ns (~85 MHz) today?
  1. Pocket's 80 MHz limit was hit when `ngvm_dcache` / `ngvm_mmu` / system bus were integrated on a single timing pass without `arm9v2`'s registered `SHORT_READ` boundary. Look at `s5`: ZAP in the **exact same cache wrapper** closes at **-0.583 ns** (~98 MHz) ([`s5 report`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/docs/consults/2026-10-23-codex-arm9v2-s5.md#L140-L154)).
  2. All 20 of `arm9v2`'s worst failing paths (-2.109 ns) are inside one single cone: **`m` / `w` / `a` tag compare -> `arm9v2_forward` -> `arm9v2_rotate` -> `u_execute.a`** ([`s5 report`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/docs/consults/2026-10-23-codex-arm9v2-s5.md#L165-L216)).
  3. Splitting that cone — by precomputing forwarding match bits in `D2` (`arm_cpu.sv:991-1018`) and moving `A`-stage forwarding (`a.dest_data`) to the `A` input mux instead of `S` — removes 5–6 logic levels (~1.4–1.6 ns) from `S -> A`, bringing `arm9v2` right down to ZAP's **-0.5 to -0.8 ns** envelope where the board runs rock-solid at 104 MHz today.
- **Cost**: 3–4 coding sessions on `arm9v2` + 2 sessions on shared memory; ~6–8 whole-core builds.
- **Risk**: Medium (forward-network refactoring must stay lockstep-clean against the 261-test suite).
- **The One Settling Measurement**: Run `sim/cpu` `crand1` (at `2:9` latency) and `gba_timer` after the `d2.writes` fix + 3-clock (then 2-clock) load pickup + `W` RAS checkpoint; `crand1 (2:9)` V2/ZAP cycle ratio must drop from **0.989** to **$\le$ 0.72**, and Sonic N / Asphalt on the board must jump from **31 M/s to $\ge$ 42 M/s** before memory tweaks.

  - **~42% of clocks** are `fifo_full == 1` (the I-cache is **hitting**, has filled the 4-entry fetch FIFO, and is waiting for the backend `issue`/`shifter`/`data_stall` to drain it).
  - **Only ~5.0–5.8% of clocks** (~350K–397K fills/s × 14 clocks) are actual I-cache misses (`o_instr_wb_stb & o_instr_wb_cyc & ~i_instr_wb_ack`).
- Meanwhile, on the **D-cache side**, there are **311K load fills/s + 332K store fills/s + 300K write-backs/s + 301K uncached accesses/s = 1.244 M transactions/s** (4.073 M bus beats/s, **72% of all CPU Wishbone traffic**), causing `data_stall` (10% of clocks directly) plus a large share of `stall_from_issue` (35%) and `stall_from_shifter` (17%) while instructions wait on `i_dc_lock` during HUM load fills!
- **Conclusion**: The **D-cache / bus merger path (72% of bus beats)** is $2.5\times$ larger on the bus than the I-cache path (28% of bus beats).

### Step-by-Step Technical Specifications for the Two Threads

#### Thread 1 (`arm9v2` CPU): How to Execute T1.1 and T1.2
1. **T1.1 — Fix the `d2.writes` WAW Bug, 3-Clock Load-Use, and `W` RAS Checkpoint (Session 6)**:
   - In [`arm9v2_decode.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_decode.sv#L54-L66), add `logic [1:0] writes;` to `d2_slot_t`, populate it in [`decode_one`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_decode.sv#L120-L380), and gate the WAW check at [`arm9v2_decode.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_decode.sv#L424-L425):
     ```systemverilog
     if (d2.writes[dst] && d2.dest[dst] == i_load_tag[pending])
         load_hazard = 1'b1;
     ```
   - Shrink `load_valid` from 6 stages to the active pre-`W` window so a dependent instruction in `D2` releases into `S` as the hit load reaches `W` (giving the 3-clock load-use schedule).
   - Carry `{ras_ptr, ras_count}` (7 bits) through `d1 -> d2 -> s -> a -> m -> w` and feed `w.ras_ptr` / `w.ras_count` on `wb_flush` at [`arm9v2_core.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_core.sv#L612-L614) instead of zeroing the stack.
2. **T1.2 — Split the `S -> A` Critical Path & Enable 2-Clock (1-Stall) Unshifted Load-Use (`afwd`)**:
   - **Why both problems have the exact same fix**:
     Right now, [`arm9v2_execute.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_execute.sv#L78-L96) forwards `A`-stage results (`a.dest_data`, which is really the `A->M` pipeline register) into `S` (`u_forward`), passes them through the barrel shifter (`arm9v2_rotate`), and registers `u_execute.a` (`S->A` register).
   - **The Pocket-style split**:
     1. In `D2` ([`arm9v2_decode.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/arm9v2/arm9v2_decode.sv)), classify each source operand `src_tag[0..3]` as **early** (needs shifter/multiplier in `S`: `src_tag[1]` when shift is not `LSL #0`, `src_tag[2]` register shift length, or `MUL` operands) vs. **late** (needed only in `A`: `src_tag[0]` `Rn`, unshifted `src_tag[1]` `Rm` with `LSL #0`, `src_tag[3]` store data / accumulate).
     2. Precompute the 6-bit tag equality flags at the `D2 -> S` register boundary (and update on `S` hold), removing the 6-bit tag comparators from the `S` combinational path.
     3. Add a small 3-input late bypass mux at the start of `A` (selecting between the `S->A` operand register, the `A->M` ALU result register, and the `M->W` load/ALU result register).
     4. Now:
        - `S` forwarding (`arm9v2_forward`) no longer needs the `A`-stage ALU producer at all (shrinking the `S` mux and cutting ~1.4 ns from `m[506] -> u_execute.a[96]`)!
        - An unshifted ALU or base-address consumer after an `LDR` stalls only **1 clock** in `D2`/`S`, enters `A` as the load enters `W` (when `tr_cache_words` is registered in [`zap_dcache.sv`](file:///Users/jacob/dev/fpga/mister/NGage_MiSTer-cx-arm9/rtl/cpu/zap/zap_dcache.sv#L417-L435)), and picks up the formatted hit load word at the `A` late bypass (**2 clocks total, 1 stall clock**)!
