## What changed

Both are zero-cycle, default 1 in `arm9v2_core`, and asserted equal to
the old form on every clock. ZAP is unchanged.

**`THUMB_HALF`** (`decode_d1`).
- **The path:** `head_select` → `o_thumb` → `raw_word`'s thumb/pc[1]
  multiplexer → Thumb decode → D1.
- **The change:** the fetch queue moves a Thumb pc[1] instruction into
  the low halfword when it pushes the entry. Decode's instruction is then
  the queue head's word, with no multiplexer. Every other use of the word
  is ARM-only.
- **This is part of the plan's E item** "capture the Thumb halfword at
  queue push". The decode hints were not done.

**`REDIRECT_EQ`** (`wb_flush`).
- **The path:** `w` → the `load_data` multiplexer (saved load / short
  word / bus word) → `return_target` → the 32-bit comparison with the
  prediction → `wb_redirect`.
- **The change:** the upper 30 bits of every source are compared in
  parallel, the source select picks one bit, and the two low bits
  follow the Thumb state.
- **The sources:** the saved load, W's short word, the bus word, the
  record address and the ALU result.

## The test

**`arm9_return_target_rtl`**: PC writes from every load-word source.
- **The sources:** a D-cache hit, a cold miss, an uncached load, LDM
  with the PC, MOV PC, and a Thumb BL call and return.
- **The pattern:** each runs four times, with the target the same and
  then alternating. The ARM targets differ in their high bits and the
  Thumb ones in bit 1 only. Every run shifts its marker into a register,
  so the order is checked.
- **The Thumb runs** are placed at both halfword positions.
- **The checks:** it passes on ZAP and V2, and its trace matches QEMU's.

**Mutants:**
- **Caught:** a return check that matches any source instead of the
  selected one (at 0:0); one that drops bit 1; and decode moving the
  pc[1] halfword twice.
- **Not caught, and harmless:** a compare against the wrong source that
  never matches. It only adds redirects.
