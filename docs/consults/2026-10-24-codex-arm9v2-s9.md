## Cut 1: store collision after formatting

The short-read port now selects a raw hit word and builds the potential
store-merged word from W's already registered data and four byte enables.
Both words are formatted in parallel. The accepted-store/same-word/way
qualifier selects the finished result. The FSM's full-line write-enable
selection no longer precedes the byte merge and load formatting. No new
pipeline edge or cache-ACK admission path is introduced.

The victim-line merge, physical RAM writes, fill handling and ZAP's long
port are unchanged. Only a live IDLE/HUM lookup can forward this store;
fill and saved uncached-store owners are separate states. Simulation
assertions check that the live store's full-line data and enables equal
the registered W payload used by the factored path. The source-RTL cache
checks pass 21/21 with those assertions enabled.

All three configurations pass 267/267 and 69 QEMU comparisons. Every cycle
and instruction pair equals session 8. All 85 raw timing intervals at
0:0 / 0:3 / 2:9, with prediction on and off, are identical; the hit windows
remain 3/4/5. All four top-level lint combinations and cpu-gen-check pass.
[Validation](arm9v2/s9-cut1-validation.json).

The seed-2 trial improves hot slack by 0.311 ns and saves 65 ALMs.
Cold slack is -2.439 ns (essentially unchanged). This pays modestly,
but does not meet the structural target; continue with return repair.
[Fit and path evidence](arm9v2/s9-cut1-seed2/manifest.json).

## Cut 2: bounded normal-return repair

Normal retirement carries a six-bit return owner instead of a 64-bit
one-hot selector. Four S and four A repair comparisons are captured on the
same edge as the return data. S compares the next admitted D2 owner or its
held owner; A compares the accepted S owner or its held owner. The lock is
still checked *after* the accepting edge, indexed by the registered return
owner, so a miss ACK that installs a lock cannot release a consumer.
Only the resulting ready bit qualifies the eight local matches.

The background C selector/data already arrive registered, require no lock
qualification, and keep their existing local operand selection. Pending
and live-producer age checks retain priority over either retired return.
Repair still wins BX/SWP/block raw operand captures; exceptions and flushes
clear the new match records. No ACK enters admission or forwarding.

All three configurations pass 267/267 and 69 QEMU comparisons, with every
cycle/instruction pair and all 85 windows equal to session 8. Source and
generated execute benches pass 68,939 checks; decode and front-end/cache
component checks pass. All four lints and cpu-gen-check pass.
[Validation](arm9v2/s9-cut2-validation.json).

Seed 2 gains another 0.132 ns hot / 0.109 ns cold, at a cost of 93 ALMs.
This pays on timing; area remains above the target.
[Fit and path evidence](arm9v2/s9-cut2-seed2/manifest.json).

## Cut 3: independent D1 destination selection

Each registered D1 slot computes the few exceptional lane-0 destinations
in parallel: a block-transfer register, multiply's Rn destination, link
register, or banked SPSR. Finished physical tags are selected with the
registered queue-head bits. The complete priority classifier still supplies
the instruction kind, but no longer controls the destination-tag mux.
The fast direct-branch path and subsequent block-list steps are unchanged.

A Yosys SAT miter proves the new destination function equals the original
kind-based selection for all combinations of instruction, Thumb class,
mode, undefined bit, list count/PC bit and first register. This includes
invalid and empty-list cases, and all bank mappings.
[Reproducible miter and proof](arm9v2/s9-destination-proof/manifest.json).

All three configurations pass 267/267 and 69 QEMU comparisons; every
cycle/instruction pair and all 85 raw windows remain identical. Decode
passes 6,586,943 checks in both source and generated forms; the cache
integration passes all three latency variants. All four lints and
cpu-gen-check pass. [Validation](arm9v2/s9-cut3-validation.json).

The D1 candidate does **not** pay at seed 2: hot slack regresses by
0.514 ns, cold by 0.536 ns, and area grows 307 ALMs. Record this passing
functional candidate and its proof, then restore the exact cut-2 RTL
before the RF experiment. No second D1 variant or seed sweep is attempted.
[Rejected fit and paths](arm9v2/s9-cut3-seed2/manifest.json).
