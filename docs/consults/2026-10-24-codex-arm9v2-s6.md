## Cut 1: registered producer eligibility

A's write mask already registers condition, fault and PC-destination
qualification, and clears on reset, flush and output consumption. S now
consumes that mask directly instead of ANDing it with A's valid bit again.
At A-to-M capture, the record's two writer bits are qualified with its
valid bit. M, W and the reservation clear their writer bits whenever their
valid bits clear, including reload, deferred-return completion and abort
recovery. The masks therefore remain ready producers throughout every hold
and transfer. S consumes M/W's registered writer bits without recomputing
valid/pass/fault eligibility. No register-data path, load scoreboard,
cache ACK, readiness check or architectural schedule changes.

The A mask and existing M/W record registers are protected against retiming.
The only forwarding network retains three producers and two lanes each.
Normal loads still are **not** ready producers; late pickup remains pending.

Full ZAP and both V2 modes again pass **264/264**, each with **68 QEMU
agreements** and the **85 unchanged window models**. Every cycle and
instruction count in all three configurations equals the merged baseline.
[The complete validation](arm9v2/s6-qualifiers-validation.json) retains that
comparison. Source/generated execute and S/A integration benches also pass.
Both emu lint configurations and `cpu-gen-check` pass. The integrated seed-2 fit is **-1.237 ns at 100C / -1.172 ns at -40C**,
**16,417 ALMs**, improving worst-slow by **0.414 ns** while adding 536 ALMs
and 486 registers. All holds remain positive (minimum +0.133 ns).
[Fit, paths and hashes](arm9v2/s6-qualifiers-seed2/manifest.json). This clears
the approximate -1.6 ns single-seed target; the other requested structural
cuts still need their own measured results. It is not three-seed or
whole-device timing closure.

The independently built merged baseline and this cut also match all **85
measured windows at each of 0:0 / 0:3 / 2:9**, in both predictor modes.
[Window measurements](arm9v2/s6-qualifiers-windows.json) preserve the final
two visits, including serial/fill variation; no model was retargeted.
The warmed LDR + dependent ADD interval is still **8 clocks**, independent
LDR + independent ADD is **7**, and predicted B + target marker is **2**
(**6** with prediction off). Late pickup and false-writer cleanup have
not been implemented.

[All 264 comparison rows](arm9v2/s6-cycles.csv) contain ZAP and both V2

## Cut 2: registered selected memory check

A compact record registers the FCSE-adjusted check address, access mode,
user override, load format and read/write enables at the existing M/W
boundary. Selection uses the owner that the existing backend transition
will expose next: incoming A on a normal advance, W on a hold or retry,
M during the reload preparation, and the reserved record on reload completion.
The D-TLB consumes the registered address and mode directly. The registered
W flush still suppresses check enables immediately, preserving older-event
priority. The RAM launch, architectural request and data paths are unchanged.

A retry or just-installed fill disables the next check until the held owner
has been read again. Deferred PC loads and SWP wait for their registered
background return as before; SWP's write phase is captured only after its
store bit changes. Block abort restoration disables younger checks. CP15/PID
changes retain the existing drain and flush. No check moves a clock.

The bench compares the registered enables to the previous state/owner
schedule on every edge, and compares address, mode, user override and format
for every active check. The reference does not drive synthesized hardware.
This makes owner alignment observable even when a speculative lookup would
not otherwise change an architectural signature. Full ZAP and both V2 modes
pass **264/264**, each with **68 QEMU agreements**, and all cycle/instruction
pairs equal the merged baseline and cut 1. Both emu lint modes and
`cpu-gen-check` pass. All 85 windows at every latency in both modes are
identical, with no model changes. [Validation](arm9v2/s6-check-validation.json)
and [windows](arm9v2/s6-check-windows.json).

The seed-2 integrated result is **-1.381 ns at 100C / -1.385 ns at -40C**,
**16,354 ALMs**. All holds are positive (minimum +0.131 ns). Against cut 1,
worst-slow is **0.148 ns worse**, area is 63 ALMs lower, and fitted registers
fall from 18,405 to 17,465. These are fitted totals, including physical
optimization, not the literal count of new RTL flops. RAM and DSP usage stay
320,928 bits / 71 M10Ks / 4 DSPs. [Fit, paths and hashes](arm9v2/s6-check-seed2/manifest.json).

**This cut does not pay in the integrated seed-2 timing result. The session
stops under the explicit no-pay rule.** Both this candidate and cut 1 are
above -2 ns and better than the approximate -1.6 ns target, but that does
not override the separate per-cut stopping instruction. No additional seed
is run to try to reverse the decision. The candidate is committed separately
with its measured regression, rather than described as a timing improvement.
The best measured session-6 source remains cut 1, `51a0123`, at -1.237 ns.

The first cut's gain over the merged baseline is 0.414 ns; this candidate's
net gain over that baseline is 0.266 ns. Neither is a three-seed median.
Relative to the supplied historical ZAP same-wrapper -0.584 ns, they are
0.653 and 0.801 ns worse respectively. The last recorded ZAP area, 16,549
ALMs, is historical: no new ZAP fit of the deferred-write-back merge was
run, so it is not a current matched-area acceptance comparison.

## Remaining measured paths and risks

The final candidate's worst-30 sets are led by the fetch queue: 29 paths
at 100C and 28 at -40C start at `head_select` bits; the remaining one/two
start at `read_pc[6]`. The worst path starts at `head_select[3]` and crosses
queue Thumb selection (fanout 151), raw-word selection and Thumb expansion
into D1. Direct-branch shortcut/redirect capture shares the queue controls.

| Candidate family | Worst 100C slack / levels / data delay | Worst -40C slack / levels / data delay | Worst-30 count 100C / -40C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Queue head -> Thumb/raw-word selection -> expansion -> D1 capture | -1.381 ns / 8 / 10.396 ns | -1.385 ns / 8 / 10.346 ns | 23 / 21 |
| Queue head -> Thumb/direct-branch shortcut -> registered redirect target | -1.352 ns / 7 / 10.272 ns | -1.343 ns / 7 / 10.261 ns | 7 / 9 |

The -40C endpoints are `u_decode.d1[1][194]` and
`u_decode.redirect_ff[63]`, with clock skew -0.484 and -0.527 ns.
The 100C worst D1 endpoint is `d1[0][27]`. This is placement and path-ranking
evidence, not proof that all unlisted families are closed. The preceding
cut-1 fit was still led by `m[93] -> S forwarding/rotation -> a[89]`,
10 levels / 10.219 ns at 100C and 11 levels / 10.156 ns at -40C. Its
worst-30 sets contain 15 / 20 instances of that family. No extra path cut
is made after the no-pay result.

Functional risk in cut 1 is keeping writer masks aligned with valid records
through holds, reservation reload, flush and abort; masks clear on each
consumption/kill and all suites retain identical behavior. Functional risk
in cut 2 is duplicating the next check-owner schedule: a future backend
transition change must update its capture too. Active-check equivalence
assertions cover the schedule, metadata and FCSE/mode alignment on every
executed test clock, including retry/fill/SWP/abort paths. This is not formal
proof or firmware/board validation. The `dont_retime` boundaries keep the
intended cuts but constrain physical optimization and can change area and
placement elsewhere, as the fits demonstrate.

## Exact stopping point

The master merge is `7b96cc8`; it preserves deferred dirty write-back and the
accepted-hit replacement update. Producer qualification is `51a0123`, with
full cycle equality and an improved seed-2 fit. The following selected-check
boundary is retained as a separately measured candidate, with its 0.148 ns
regression documented above. The working source at the end includes that
candidate; `51a0123` remains the better measured timing alternative.

The **third cut is not implemented**: W destination still selects the
background-load lock/return bit before retirement qualification. The proposed
bounded change is to register its one-hot destination alongside W, then
reduce the lock/return vectors against that selector, keeping readiness out
of S and D2 admission. It has no fit or acceptance claim in this session.

The **cycle-model work is not implemented**: true destination-writer masks,
three-clock scalar-load pickup, registered permission-qualified W load
forwarding, retirement replay for misses/retries/holds, and W RAS checkpoint
restoration all remain pending. Existing LDM protection is intact. The
LDR + dependent ADD proof interval remains eight clocks, not five or six.
The current all-test ratios and 85-window measurements are baseline evidence,
not acceptance of the requested faster load model. The random-workload gain
at 0:3 is modest and at 2:9 is only about 1% for crand/ctrand; the phone-speed
goal is not demonstrated.

The next session needs the structural decision at this stopping point before
building the cycle model: keep the check boundary despite its single-seed
regression, or resume from the better measured producer-qualifier cut. Keep
the one forwarding network and the registered cache data/permission producer;
never qualify D2 issue from cache ACK. A scalar replay must preserve abort
priority and kill younger speculative work before retirement, while a partial
LDM must retain its protection rather than skipping remaining transfers.
