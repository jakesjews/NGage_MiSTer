# ARM9V2 session 3: decode timing and cold redirects

The first milestone is complete: frontend/decode median worst-slow slack
improves from -0.890 to **-0.210 ns**, and hot untrained direct branches lose
**three slots instead of six**. Predicted branches still lose zero slots.
Median area rises from 4,159 to 4,666 ALMs. ZAP remains default; V2 still has
no architectural retirement. CPU comparisons protect existing implementations
and are not claims about V2 game speed. S/A work follows this milestone.

## Goal and scope

The goal is matching the phone's games on the board: Asphalt's 21 fps cap,
smooth Tony Hawk and Sonic's teens-to-twenties. The 47–48 M instructions/s
estimate comes from Asphalt's approximately 2.3 M instructions/frame. It is
a yardstick for that outcome. Firmware-invisible implementation choices may
change; the observable ISA, CP15 identity, exceptions, MMU and timers remain.
A CPU/component fit does not establish whole-device timing or gameplay speed.

## Structural pass before S/A

The starting point is session 2 commit `7f42e7a`. Its frontend/decode/RF/S-entry
component had median worst-slow setup slack -0.890 ns and 4,159 median ALMs.
Section 1 of the Antigravity consultation and all three session-2 worst-path
sets were reviewed before editing. The named families were:

| Seed / worst corner | Shared family | Levels | Data delay ns | Skew ns |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 / -40C | queue head, fanout 256 → queue mux → halfword select → Thumb expansion → D1 | 7 | 9.608 | -0.534 |
| 3 / -40C | queue head, fanout 255 → queue mux → list count → D1 | 6 | 10.087 | -0.510 |
| 4 / -40C | queue head → queue mux → list count → D1 | 6 | 10.233 | -0.102 |

The worst 30 paths grouped as Thumb/list/branch-target were 11/1/18,
2/24/4 and 0/30/0. D1 head → D2 target also reached 9.890 ns at seven
levels in seed 2 and 10.137 ns in seed 3. These are data delays; setup
slack also includes clock skew and endpoint requirements.

The pass replaces the fetch queue's binary head with a registered rotating
one-hot selector. Selection remains entirely over stored raw words and
metadata; cache hit/permission status never bypasses the queue into decode.
List population count uses three six-input truth tables, a carry-save
reduction and one five-bit sum. The first-bit removal uses parallel prefix
masks instead of a subtract-and-mask chain. Complete ARM, Thumb, prefix and
suffix branch sums are computed in parallel before selecting the target.

Cold direct branches had three avoidable handoffs. The registered redirect
now launches the cache lookup on its applying edge instead of first copying
its target into the sequential-PC register. An unpredicted direct branch
emits its registered redirect when entering D2, preserving that D2 owner
through the subsequent younger-record kill. With D1 empty, simple
unconditional ARM B/BL or Thumb B can enter D2 directly from the registered
fetch queue using dedicated trivial controls and target sums. Other
instructions retain the ordinary D1 path and single Thumb expander. This
shortcut is a measured latency choice, not a cache-response bypass.

Correct predictions do not redirect. Predicted wrong-target branches still
validate against the registered target at D2/S. Such an older late redirect
wins over a younger early event accepted at the same edge. Older backend
flush wins over both. A blocked S slot retains the early-redirect owner in
D2 until it can issue, without duplicating the pulse. During an outstanding
cache lookup, the existing killed/drain/restart path still preserves the
request until acknowledgement; immediate target launch applies only when
the cache pipeline can advance. Faulted words never take the shortcut.

## Functional and cycle evidence

The real short-read I-cache/TLB loop at waits 0/3/9 now measures **four clocks
between hot untrained direct branches**, three lost slots instead of six.
Correctly trained branches still enter S every clock, with zero lost slots.

| Component bench | Before clocks, waits 0/3/9 | After clocks, waits 0/3/9 |
|---|---|---|
| Frontend + decode + real I-cache/TLB | 442 / 649 / 1,105 | 403 / 611 / 1,067 |
| Frontend + real I-cache/TLB | 517 / 961 / 1,849 | 515 / 959 / 1,847 |
| Hot untrained direct-branch spacing | 7 / 7 / 7 | 4 / 4 / 4 |
| Hot trained direct-branch spacing | 1 / 1 / 1 | 1 / 1 / 1 |

Standalone decode passes source and generated RTL: 6,037,278 checks each,
722,083 bench clocks, including all 65,535 nonempty lists, bank selections,
addressing modes, Thumb implicit registers and paired BL halves. New temporal
checks observe every registered redirect pulse, verify exactly one event per
miss, and cover held owners, older flush priority and simultaneous older
mistarget/younger early redirects. Integration also covers held requests,
backpressure, source locks, translation faults and redirect during fill.

The same three-slot untrained cadence also passes an upper-halfword Thumb
loop at all three bus waits. Adding that loop changes the final integration
bench totals to 501 / 781 / 1,381 clocks (1,280 / 1,852 / 3,052 checks).
The table above is the identical-stimulus before/after comparison taken
before adding this coverage; the larger final totals must not be interpreted
as a regression. Generic pipeline latency, conditional mistargets and paired
Thumb BL refill costs still need separate windows as S/A is connected.

## Timing correction within the pass

The first seed-2 trial of the redirect shortcut was -1.105 ns worst slow,
4,472 ALMs. All its worst 30 paths ended at the redirect payload register.
The queue-to-Thumb-target path had six reported logic levels but two serial
carry chains: PC + displacement, then +4. Its worst data delay was 10.442 ns
with -0.108 ns skew. The queue selector fanout had fallen from 255–256 to
115, but putting the PC bias after a full sum consumed that saving.

The corrected shortcut uses carry-save XOR/majority logic for PC,
displacement and bias, then one carry-propagating sum. A separate nine-level
D1 path was full instruction classification → branch-kind selection → target
selection → redirect payload (10.285 ns). Target selection now uses shallow
raw branch/prefix/suffix class bits rather than the complete ISA classifier.
The functional event priority, stage boundaries and cold/predicted cadence
are unchanged. Directed tests add 4,096 ARM and 4,096 Thumb targets, signed
negative displacements, both Thumb lanes and PC wraparound. Final decode
coverage is 6,061,854 checks / 746,659 clocks for source and generated RTL.
The earlier 6,037,278-check run is retained as pre-correction evidence.

## Final frontend/decode fit

All final seeds use identical RTL/netlist, the same 104 MHz constraints and
5CSEBA6U23I7 device as session 2. Only server Quartus 17.0.2 was used.

| Version | Seed | Slow 100C ns | Slow -40C ns | Worst slow ns | ALMs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Session 2 | 2 | -0.696 | -0.697 | -0.697 | 4,142 |
| Session 2 | 3 | -0.936 | -1.152 | -1.152 | 4,186 |
| Session 2 | 4 | -0.372 | -0.890 | -0.890 | 4,159 |
| Session 3 corrected pass | 2 | -0.811 | -0.698 | -0.811 | 4,657 |
| Session 3 corrected pass | 3 | -0.210 | -0.196 | -0.210 | 4,667 |
| Session 3 corrected pass | 4 | -0.112 | -0.172 | -0.172 | 4,666 |

Median worst-slow improves **0.680 ns**; individual changes are -0.114,
+0.942 and +0.718 ns. The component still does not close 104 MHz, but all
three seeds are within 1 ns and above the -2 ns structural stop threshold.
No seed was substituted. Minimum hold is +0.156 / +0.164 / +0.145 ns.
Registers are 5,354 / 5,349 / 5,339, with unchanged two M10Ks / 15,360 bits
and no DSPs. Median area increases 507 ALMs (12.2% of this component).

The hierarchy attributes most of the increase to decode. For seed 3,
decode including RF/Thumb grows 2,835.4 → 3,302.2 ALMs; fetch grows
1,109.9 → 1,128.4. Decode-local logic grows 1,926.7 → 2,426.5 ALMs,
including the independent shortcut payload/target path, parallel sums and
new list mapping. The RF and predictor have no architectural change; their
small packing changes do not explain the increase. This is not a full CPU
area comparison against ZAP, so the eventual same-cache <=5% CPU allowance
has not yet been demonstrated.

| Final worst path | Levels | Data delay ns | Skew ns | Worst-30 groups |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Seed 2 / 100C: D1 `head`, fanout 330 → D1 select → classify → source tag → `d2[107]` | 8 | 9.730 | -0.526 | 28 classification/tags, 2 shortcut target |
| Seed 3 / 100C: D1 `head` → classify/destination select → `d2[78]` | 9 | 9.548 | -0.107 | 30 classification/tags |
| Seed 4 / -40C: fetch `head_select[7]` → queue/Thumb → `d1~401` | 7 | 9.510 | -0.107 | 30 queue/Thumb |

The list arithmetic and D1 branch-target chains no longer dominate. Thumb
expansion remains the worst family in seed 4, though its component slack
improves 0.718 ns from that seed's prior list path. The next integration must
preserve the registered S entrance and avoid turning D1 tag selection into
a forwarding/admission path. More local D1 selector replication or shallower
classification are possible later cuts; no additional timing round was made
for those remaining families before the S/A milestone.

The preliminary shortcut trial also completed seed 3 at -1.020 ns / 4,458
ALMs. It is retained with seed 2 as diagnostic evidence, not a three-seed
result. Exact netlists, four-corner worst-30 paths, area, constraints and
source hashes are under `arm9v2/s3-pass*-seed*` and the matching trial files.
No whole-device or firmware-boot build was run.

Concurrency was checked before launches. A cross-thread launch race briefly
produced five global Quartus containers while two were ours. The seed-4
container was stopped immediately on detection, restoring four, then rerun
only after seed 3 finished. The interrupted fit is not a timing observation.
Completed/stopped containers use `--rm`; no session container remains after
this milestone. Shared images and other threads' jobs were not removed.

## Milestone stopping point

Frontend/decode RTL and its verification are ready for the S/A milestone.
S/A execution, M/W, precise retirement and complete V2 workload cycles remain
unimplemented at this commit. No D-cache or sys files were changed. The next
work is the single registered-producer forwarding network and rotator in S,
registered ALU/flags/multiplier completion in A, and standalone/joined tests
and three-seed fit. The full CPU and gameplay gates remain open.
