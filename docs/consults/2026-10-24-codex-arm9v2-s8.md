# ARM9V2 session 8: retained scalar-load consumers and first firmware

Starting at `4616d9c`, on codex/arm9v2; ZAP remains default. No master merge.

## Recovery baseline

Bench-only counters classify scalar retirement recovery as (1) no first
short hit without a prior W wait, (2) held/retried without a short hit,
(3) eventual short hit after waiting. ZAP's existing replay bit is class 4.
The first class is not a bus-miss counter: the D-cache event counters remain
the source for cache fill/uncached transaction counts. Killed slots count
valid queue and backend uop slots, not deduplicated architectural instructions.
Refill clocks run from registered recovery to the next retirement, including
memory wait; they are not an exclusive frontend-stall CPI component.

All six requested tests at 0:3 and 2:9 pass on both cores, and every
cycle/instruction pair equals session 7. ZAP has zero replay events here.
[Raw measurements](arm9v2/s8-recovery-baseline.json).

At 2:9, cache has 24,662 recoveries, 315,049 killed slots and 279,692
clocks until retirement resumes. crand1 has 297 recoveries, 2,523 killed
slots and 8,142 refill clocks; 128 of the recoveries are eventual short hits.
These are observed occupancy costs, not an estimate of recoverable total cycles.

## Retained scalar completion

Scalar-load completion no longer requests a W flush. A registered operand
lock follows a released consumer into S/A. S waits for early operands;
A waits for late operands. A first permission-qualified short hit retains
the raw 3/4/5 independent/late/early windows. An unavailable word is never
selected. A registered normal completion or the cache's existing registered
background port C repairs the held operand, which survives subsequent holds.
The normal completion is qualified against the lock installed by its ACK:
a fill ACK is not usable data. No ACK is added to D2/S admission or forwarding.

D2 carries unresolved W ownership across the same edge that installs a cache
lock. Without it, dcache_rtl at 0:3 failed check 23, PC 0x7ac: a comparison
executed before the background return. WAW admission remains conservative,
except when a true source dependency already protects the overwrite or W
has a known hit. Abort/exception priority, partial LDM, SWP and PC loads keep
their prior protection. Independent work may retire with a background lock;
dhum_rtl at 2:9 records 1,441 such uops.

A held access can resume without the two lookup-reload clocks when both
reserved younger records are non-memory operations. Memory records still
use the original reload. An initial shortcut exposed a same-edge operand
repair priority bug: align at 0:3/2:9 branched to 0x98 instead of 0x950,
after BX at 0x94c. BX's raw Rm assignment overrode the repaired word. The
repair now wins all class-specific captures, including BX/SWP and the first
block base. Directed execute checks cover held background completion, an
early shifted consumer, and BX completion on its admission edge.

All three configurations pass **264/264 and 68 QEMU references**. ZAP's
cycle/instruction pairs are exactly unchanged. V2's instruction counts change
